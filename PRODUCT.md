# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: Semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS with custom design tokens, and lightweight vanilla JS for high performance, smooth animations, and zero-framework overhead.

## Users
Young people, couples, groups of friends, and food lovers in Fernando de la Mora, Asunción, and Central seeking premium gourmet burgers (blends de picaña y costilla), legendary lomitos, pizzas, craft drinks, and an energetic night-out atmosphere with live music.

## Product Purpose
Official landing page and digital menu for **ED'S House Fernando**, allowing customers to easily explore the gourmet menu with real pricing, place orders via WhatsApp or PedidosYa, find location & directions on Google Maps, check opening hours (opens 18:00 hs), and discover the 12+ year culinary legacy.

## Positioning
Bar, lomitería y restaurante gourmet urbano: more than a decade (12+ years) serving high-end picaña/rib burger blends, gratin-baked Lomitos & Árabes Extreme, and giant Lomipizzas with ice-cold draft beer (Chopp Heineken) and live music in an electric nightlife setting.

## Operating Context
Evening & night visits (opens at 6:00 p.m. / 18:00 hs). High mobile traffic from patrons deciding dinner with friends or ordering late-night takeaway/delivery. Demands instant WhatsApp contact, clear pricing in Guaraníes (₲), and reliable GPS navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Showcase signature burgers (Picaña Classic, Americana, Doble Picaña, BBQ), legendary Lomitos & Árabes, Entradas, Lomipizzas, and Pizzas con borde relleno.
- Real pricing in Guaraníes (₲) as verified from authentic menu cartas.
- Instant direct WhatsApp ordering/reservations linking to Fernando de la Mora (`+595 971 906438` / `+595 984 525513`).
- Google Maps interactive embed & directions to Músicos del Chaco, Fernando de la Mora.
- Authentic customer social proof (4.4★ with 85+ Google reviews).
- Fast, mobile-first responsive layout with accessible semantic markup and high-contrast typography.

## Brand Commitments
- Name/Identity: ED'S House (o Edwards House) - Sucursal Fernando de la Mora.
- Tone: Bold, passionate, urban, vibrant, friendly, and deeply appetizing.
- Signature Assets: Official circular red/yellow ED'S House logo, authentic food photos and printed menu cards.
